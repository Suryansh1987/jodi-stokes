# 3. Program and Registration Experience

Status: Not Started  
Depends On: Task 1

## Goal

Replace the three mock programs and fake cohort application with an accurate Unstoppable You presentation and a minimal, accessible initial registration experience.

## Context

- `ProgramsSection` maps three mock `Program` records and renders visual-only add-to-cart buttons.
- `CoachingSection` already provides a responsive split layout and accessible labels, but it displays a fake 12-week cohort, date, seat count, pricing schedule, features, and a local-only application confirmation.
- Header/About/Hero CTAs currently target either `#programs` or `#coaching`.
- No approved exact agreement/waiver text exists in the repository.

## Files / Areas Likely Involved

- `components/sections/home/programs-section.tsx`
- `components/sections/home/coaching-section.tsx` (prefer a clear rename such as `program-registration-section.tsx` if its role changes)
- `app/(public)/page.tsx`
- `lib/content/home.ts`
- `lib/content/programs-products.json`
- `lib/content/programs-products.ts`
- Program/coaching/form selectors in `app/globals.css`
- Program/form assertions in `test/home.spec.ts`

## Required Changes

- Render exactly one primary program, Unstoppable You, from centralized content.
- Remove Strong Foundations, Lean & Lifted, The Reset, all add-to-cart language, fake cohort dates, seat counts, community claims, bloodwork claims, guarantees, and unsupplied features.
- Keep an appropriate existing card/media layout, but adapt it to one featured offer instead of preserving an artificial three-card grid.
- Use `Register` as the program CTA and link it to a stable registration section/form anchor.
- Reuse the current Coaching split-panel/form styles where practical; rename the component and IDs if that makes its registration purpose clearer.
- Registration fields: required full name, email, phone, selected program, and agreement acknowledgement; optional message/fitness goals.
- Default the selected program by stable ID to Unstoppable You. Keep the select/data flow capable of another JSON program record later.
- Present only client-approved agreement/waiver information. Require an acknowledgement checkbox whose label states acknowledgement/read status without claiming it is the formal signed waiver.
- Clearly and succinctly state that Jodi handles formal signed paperwork and any medical disclosure privately after initial registration, using approved wording.
- Do not collect medications, diagnoses, orthopedic limitations, or other sensitive medical history.
- In this task, keep submission local/non-successful or explicitly marked pending Task 4; do not display a false “received” confirmation before email delivery exists.
- Add accessibility-first Playwright coverage for headings, labels, required controls, program selection, acknowledgement, CTA focus/scroll behavior, and absent sensitive fields.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- No email provider or server endpoint; Task 4 owns delivery.
- No calendar, scheduling widget, coaching payment, signed-document platform, medical intake, database, CRM, or authentication.
- Do not publish the older private-training prices or group examples as separate programs.
- Do not draft legal text or infer an ideal client, outcome, guarantee, transformation, date, or capacity claim.
- Do not touch Journal or Newsletter.

## Data / Client Requirements

- Use the confirmed Unstoppable You facts recorded in `taskTracker.md`, preserving approximate qualifiers.
- Exact public marketing copy for hormones, metabolism, nutrition, measurement/weigh-in, YouTube access, and capacity needs client approval.
- Exact approved agreement/waiver copy is a blocker for completing the acknowledgement content.
- The old private-training agreement is supporting source material, not permission to publish all rates or offers.

## Edge Cases

- With one program, the select may be read-only/disabled visually but its stable program ID still must submit; do not rely on disabled controls, which are omitted from form submissions.
- Agreement acknowledgement must reset predictably after a successful submission once Task 4 is wired.
- Native validation should handle missing required fields before any network request.
- Error/status content needs an `aria-live` region and must not shift or overlap the form.
